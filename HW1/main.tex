\section{Interpretation and limitations}
\PureVisualConclusion{}
Losses, augmentation, and budget change together, confounding causal attribution.
\PureArchitectureConfound{}
Reconstruction monitors are train-seen, including through the initializer;
test images were observed during development.

One seed and 512 samples limit statistical conclusions.
This Torchvision/Ignite protocol is not numerically interchangeable with
TensorFlow-FID or Clean-FID. ImageNet features and visual plausibility do not
establish anatomical correctness. Verified patient-separated validation,
repeated runs, and matched-budget loss comparisons remain future work.
